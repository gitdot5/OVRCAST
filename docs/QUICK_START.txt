OVRCAST 0.3.2 — Public preview

Your AI capacity, at a glance.
Created by Git. Free and open source under the MIT license.

1. Unzip the download, drag OVRCAST into Applications, then open it.
2. In Connect, install/open the free CodexBar companion if it is not detected.
3. Connect your provider accounts in CodexBar, select the providers to track,
   then click Check connection and Open my board.

The built app needs no Python, development folder or Terminal setup.
CodexBar remains a separate dependency. Account sign-in stays with your provider.

Click the pin to keep the board open. Drag its title bar to move it.
Command-Shift-U shows or hides the board. The eye button turns on privacy mode.
Settings contains aliases, notifications, quiet hours and computer connections.

To add another Mac, install OVRCAST there and use an existing trusted SSH
alias in Computers. OVRCAST does not configure Remote Login or SSH keys.
Both apps must be running to keep their snapshots fresh.

Download OVRCAST-0.3.2-macOS.zip from the official GitHub release. It is signed under
Du Nguyen Tran's Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Older preview artifacts
are separate builds. Do not disable macOS security to install software.

OVRCAST is free and MIT licensed. Original source and license notices are
included in the source distribution. Provider services may have their own fees.

Official download: https://github.com/gitdot5/OVRCAST/releases/tag/v0.3.2
Test scope and limitations: docs/RELEASE_REVIEW.md in the source distribution.

Account cards show the next provider-reported usage reset, including the window,
countdown and exact local date/time with time zone. Open an account for each
reported quota schedule. Missing reset times stay unavailable; a passed reset
waits for a new provider reading before confirming allowance.
