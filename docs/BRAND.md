# OVRCAST

**Your AI capacity, at a glance.**

Created by Git. Free and open source under the MIT license.

The name is always written **OVRCAST**, in uppercase. Earlier local builds were
named Usage Board. Existing data paths, SSH collection and preference identifiers
retain their original names for compatibility.

## Product identity

Use **OVRCAST** as the product name and the segmented capacity-ring emblem as its
app icon. Three account segments share one ring on a warm ivory native macOS tile.
Dark arcs represent remaining allowance; pale arcs complete each account track.
The ring forms an O for OVRCAST and brings multiple allowances into one view. Keep the same name and icon
in the menu bar app, About screen, documentation and downloadable packages.

Describe the app as: “A local macOS menu bar app for tracking AI allowances,
resets and reported credits across accounts and Macs.”

The interface should be compact and calm, with Day and Night appearances,
readable numbers and clear source status. Pair warning colors with text.
Use “remaining” consistently. Show unavailable data as unknown, never zero.

## Attribution

OVRCAST is an independent project. CodexBar provides the MIT source used by OVRCAST’s bundled, maintained collector and inspired parts of the allowance display.
Keep that credit visible in About and in the README. Preserve the license and
third-party notices in source archives and app bundles.

Provider names identify supported services; they do not indicate endorsement.
Public packages include the original OVRCAST icon and the three provider
identification marks documented in `assets/README.md`. Private development
assets extracted from other applications remain excluded.
